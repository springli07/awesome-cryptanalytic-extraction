## Normal Alignment: New Preprint

[Normal Alignment: Improved Cryptanalytic Sign Recovery on Hard-Label Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18751), Shi Tang, Zirui Chen, Yongjia Su, Zhengchao Gao, Lingyue Qin and Xiaoyang Dong. Submitted September 16, 2026; v1, 34 pages. Category: hard-label extraction.

Normal-signature alignment and the combined **eSOE + Alignment** method improve the sign-recovery stage. The paper reports 512/512 signs recovered on its CIFAR-10 network (192-64×8-10), and 320/320 on its MNIST network (64-96×3-32-10). Its comparison estimates that the earlier sign method would need enumeration over **2^52** or **2^82** sign assignments. These are estimated enumeration burdens, not completed baseline runs of that size.

This is not a demonstrated complete end-to-end extraction pipeline: signature collection/recovery and the remaining model-reconstruction stages must not be counted as accomplished simply because all signs were recovered. The reported cases also do not independently establish a universal practical guarantee for every deep network. PDF p.5 announces experimental source code but supplies `XXX`, so the catalog marks it **announced, URL placeholder**.

中文：新增仅标签深层符号恢复预印本。报告模型分别恢复全部 512/512 和 320/320 个符号；原方法的 2^52、2^82 是论文估计的枚举规模，不是已经运行完的基线。不能将这一符号恢复结果表述为完整端到端模型提取。源码地址仍为占位符。

## ePrint 2026/848: Major Revision

[Cryptanalytic Extraction of Neural Networks for Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/848), Wen Zhang, Bingsheng Zhang, Tianpei Lu and Kui Ren. Latest revision September 17, 2026; 52 pages, still a preprint. Former title: **PPML Is More Vulnerable to Cryptanalytic Extraction Attacks**. The stable ePrint identifier is unchanged; this is not an additional paper.

The revision distinguishes single-point and multi-point recovery, adds projection-based aggregation for expansive networks, and studies optimal sign-probing directions under Gaussian downstream coefficients or sufficiently wide networks. Evaluation covers eight CIFAR-10 DNNs and eight expansive networks. It reports **30%-74% fewer sign-recovery queries relative to Neuron Wiggle** on its benchmark, with 100% sign accuracy there. This is not a 30%-74% reduction for every extraction stage or every architecture. Finite-ring/fixed-point wraparound remains the relevant PPML leakage mechanism; no cryptographic key-recovery claim follows.

The [paper-linked anonymous code](https://anonymous.4open.science/r/PPML_Model_Extraction_Attack) remains accessible: the file-list API returned HTTP 200 on September 18. Accessibility does not establish that the code already implements every new result in the revision; it was not executed.

中文：已有 PPML 论文改题并实质修订，新增扩张网络实验、多点投影聚合和最优符号探测方向分析。30%-74% 是文中基准相对 Neuron Wiggle 的**符号恢复阶段查询降幅**，不能扩大为全流程或所有网络的保证。原始论文标识不变，只更新条目；匿名代码可访问，但未验证其是否覆盖所有新增实验。
