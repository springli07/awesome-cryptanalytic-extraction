## Method scope and revisions / 方法范围与修订

**Beyond Scalar Probes** recovers row-side and complementary column-side signature information from rank-one Jacobian differences across adjacent cells. Section 4 and Section 5 (PDF pp.15–26) explicitly restrict the contribution to a signature-estimation primitive. Experiments use one-hidden-layer ReLU models, 784 inputs, widths 8/32, random and MNIST-trained weights, and simulated float64/float32/float16. Table 1 reports 1,570 full-vector calls for J-vector-fixed **after an estimated critical point is supplied**. It excludes critical-point acquisition and full extraction. Bias recovery is a normalized hyperplane offset, not the physical bias. Float16 usable-transition rates are only 2.4%/1.1% (random/trained); accepted-trial error medians are not global recovery rates. Section 6 leaves deeper backward extraction and a complete bidirectional procedure open. / 签名、归一化偏置与完整参数恢复不同；不将固定临界点之后的查询数当作完整攻击预算。

**Unknown-architecture MLP v2** was compared with v1, not just relabeled. Its presentation distinguishes the zero-suffix signature route and equality-pattern sign route, and separates identifying the second-to-last layer (fixed output coefficients, §7.1) from recovering its dimension (global linear relation, §7.2 / Proposition 2). Table 1's dagger-marked rows adopt true signs; they are not complete black-box runs. The MNIST signature and sign routes have separate costs, already retained in the baseline comparison. Neither the normalized parameter error nor a finite-sample residual should be read as an unconditional global-equivalence certificate. This update makes these qualifications visible in the catalog and preserves the verified v2 fingerprint. / v2 的结构识别、层宽恢复及使用真实符号的实验分别记述。

**End-to-end hard-label extraction** retains the arXiv v1 scope: cosine/projection sign recovery reuses acquired intersection spaces. No dedicated queries applies to the sign stage, with upstream signature acquisition still charged. The trained width-16, four/six-hidden-layer demonstrations omit dead/almost-dead neurons; sampled label agreement is not certified equivalence. **Normal Alignment** remains a sign-recovery result, not a new full end-to-end demonstration. **Unknown-architecture CNN** remains architecture recovery conditional on upstream parameter recovery; two-convolution end-to-end and three-convolution layer-wise experiments are distinct. / 符号、签名、结构、部分层及全流程不能互相替换。

**Extraction meets Distillation** is placed under the existing stronger-oracle systems category. Section 3.4 / Table 1 (PDF pp.13–14) discloses logits/gradients, hidden activations and true victim weights/biases used by the evaluated attack. Later label-based reconstruction does not erase that access. Section 7.8 (pp.35–36) separates per-input labels from heterogeneous privileged reads: phase 1 uses 704,964–12,027,750 labels per seeded run; phase 3 uses 57,000–211,024 joint-fit or 29,024–96,024 split-fit repeated requests across the synthetic victims. These ranges do not form a single total budget, and 15 batch calls is not 15 input-level queries. Section 7's 14 min 19 s is a six-victim batch with both search arms/reporting, not full extraction time. The complete reconstructed classifier and high synthetic agreement do not identify the exact parameters. The two CIFAR agreements remain 56.40% and 58.29%. The first-page note says the working draft expands a CSCML 2026 paper; no proceedings DOI, pages or venue record for this version were inferred. / 有内部信息辅助的部分方向恢复及学习重建不列为纯硬标签完整提取。
