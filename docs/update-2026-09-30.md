## Search and verification / 检索与验证

Queries included `"cryptanalytic extraction" 2026 neural networks`, September 2026 extraction, recent ePrint extraction, and exact-title searches for the two arXiv papers. Primary archive pages and all three PDFs were reviewed. Previously listed GLU, Normal Alignment, PPML, unknown-architecture MLP and max-pooling CNN papers were retained without duplicate additions. This is a targeted omission audit, not an exhaustive survey of every model-stealing paper. / 此次为定向遗漏审计，不声称穷尽所有模型窃取文献。

PDF page counts, byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `data/papers.json`; downloaded PDFs and extracted text stay outside the repository. The ePrint PDF required a `?download=1` request after the plain URL returned HTTP 403; its canonical PDF URL is retained. / 不将第三方论文 PDF 纳入仓库。

The generated English/Chinese tables and BibTeX are checked with `render_catalog.py --check` and `validate_catalog.py`. The validation script also checks this audit and templates for broken local links and malformed Unicode. Remote availability checks cover the new archive/PDF sources and paper-linked code repository; they do not imply execution or reproduction of research results. Historical audit documents retain their original dates and counts.
